## UI & Styling Guidelines (VentureRoot)

Our website aims for best user-friendly visibility: simple, yet polished, attractive, and a PROFESSIONAL premium design. It should NOT look naive, AI-generated, or "vibe coded".

**Core Spacing & Proportions (derived from Dashboard):**
- **Page Outer Padding:** Use balanced padding (`p-4 md:p-6 lg:p-8`) to frame the canvas cleanly.
- **Inter-card Gaps:** Use `gap-6` (approx. 24px or 8cm equivalent) for grids and card stacks to give cards clear distinction and breathing room.
- **Card Internal Padding:** Use `p-6 lg:p-7` (approx. 24px–28px). Content should take up ~70% space inside the card with comfortable framing, rather than pushing up against the borders (~80-90%).
- **Vertical Margins:** Use `mb-5` to `mb-6` for headers so text hierarchy is clean and distinct.
- **Composition:** Maintain symmetry, proportions, and visual rhythm. Avoid claustrophobic layouts and avoid dead whitespace; keep cards balanced and readable.
