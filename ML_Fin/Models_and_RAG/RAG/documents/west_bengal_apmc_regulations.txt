SECTION 1: APMC MARKET REGISTRATION & LICENSING
Under the West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, any trader, commission agent, processor, or bulk buyer operating within notified Regulated Market Committee (RMC / APMC) market yards (such as Bankura RMC, Bishnupur RMC, Medinipur RMC, Burdwan RMC) must hold a valid operating license issued by the concerned Regulated Market Committee.

SECTION 2: MARKET FEES & LEVIES
1. Market Fee Rate: A uniform market fee of 1.0% on the sale value of notified agricultural produce (e.g., Potato, Rice/Paddy, Mustard, Vegetables, Jute) is payable by the buyer/purchaser.
2. Exemption: Direct purchase from farmers outside market yards under contract farming agreements is subject to statutory exemptions as per state rules.

SECTION 3: TRADING PROCEDURES & QUALITY GRADING
All produce brought into APMC yards must undergo electronic/open auction or price discovery process. Quality grading is conducted based on AGMARK standards for moisture content, foreign matter, and size grading.
