SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
The Credit Guarantee Scheme (CGS) for Micro and Small Enterprises was launched to make available collateral-free credit to the micro and small enterprise sector. Both existing and new enterprises are eligible to be covered under the scheme.

SECTION 2: GUARANTEE COVERAGE & LOAN LIMITS
1. Maximum Credit Facility: Up to Rs. 5 crore per borrower unit (enhanced from Rs. 2 crore).
2. Extent of Guarantee Cover:
   - Up to 85% for micro enterprises for credit up to Rs. 5 lakh.
   - Up to 85% for women entrepreneurs / SC-ST entrepreneurs / MSEs in North East Region (NER).
   - Up to 75% for general category credit facilities up to Rs. 5 crore.

SECTION 3: ELIGIBLE INSTITUTIONS
Scheduled Commercial Banks (Public and Private), Select Regional Rural Banks, Small Finance Banks, and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) registered as Member Lending Institutions (MLIs).
